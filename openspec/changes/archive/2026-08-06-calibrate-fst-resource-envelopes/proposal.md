## Why

Current defaults are calibrated mainly from one Aweti final net. They do not measure transient RSS, cumulative work, opaque-operation cliffs, or apply-time nondeterminism across realistic grammar scales.

## What Changes

- Sweep entries, templates, slots, alpha variables/classes, partitions, tables, strata, rules, compounds, and word ambiguity.
- Binary-search each vector's safe cliff in supervised child processes.
- Record elapsed time, sampled peak RSS and interval, artifact bytes, states/arcs, paths/candidates,
  platform, profile, and seed.
- Version conservative default envelopes from measured evidence.

## Impact

This calibrates policy only after every frozen-model construct change, production cascade wiring,
semantic correctness gate, and worker watchdog has merged. It does not weaken correctness or
silently skip over-budget grammars.
