## Why

Circumfixes, null-role/truncating inputs, and multi-action outputs cross the morphology emitter and
preexpansion boundary. They need individual dispositions rather than one broad “affix process” claim.

## What Changes

- Witness and compile or route circumfix, null-role, and output-action variants individually.
- Preserve multi-part morpheme identity through proposer and confirmation.
- Keep Modify/InsertContext and other non-emittable actions honest at their actual boundary.

## Impact

This closes a bounded family of morphology rows without changing HermitCrab features or forcing
confirm-only behavior into native FST semantics.
