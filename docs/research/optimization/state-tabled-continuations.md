# state-tabled-continuations

Ledger entry (format: `.claude/skills/optimization-research/references/ledger-format.md`). Back to the [ledger](README.md). Research draft of 2026-11-27; the census described in the status was run, nothing else here was.

- **Status:** measured (census 2, 2026-11-27); high risk, needs an owner decision before any form that keeps a table, see the policy section in the ledger. The measurement-only census (an env-gated counter, output identical with it on and off on Aweti, Amharic, Tamil, Sena and five light Mbugwe words, 0 push mismatches in over 1.4M repeat comparisons) gives the share of stratum work under repeat arrivals of an equal state key: 0.59 on the heavy Bantu grammar (ceiling 2.45x of that work; class allomorph-on-trail 79% of it, rule order 14%), 0.60 on Aweti (ceiling 2.51x; rule order 78%), 0.07 on a second Bantu grammar (1.08x), about 0.001 on a Dravidian grammar and 0.00 on Swahili. The census is analysis-only (it counts one stratum `analyze` call: rules, template batteries, cascades and the fold; not lookup or synthesis) and a lower bound at the two hook nodes, because repeats inside one cascade or one battery are invisible.
- **Census 2 results (measured, work units; analysis ceiling / whole-parse ceiling).** Twins only: heavy Bantu 1.90x / 1.25x (8 words), ordinary 1.27x / 1.09x (80 words); Aweti 1.12x / 1.10x; second Bantu 1.02x / 1.01x; Tamil, Amharic, Swahili 1.00x. All classes (the stratum table): heavy Bantu 2.58x / 1.35x, ordinary 1.44x / 1.14x; Aweti 2.51x / 2.09x (the fourth grammar; its words are small, 163k work per word on average); second Bantu 1.08x / 1.07x; Amharic 1.01x; Tamil, Swahili 1.00x. Instructions are smaller than work: an analysis-side replay on ordinary Bantu words is worth about 1.06x of instructions (estimate).
- **Why the allomorph class dominates ("twins").** The prefixes of the heavy Bantu grammar have conditioned allomorphs with different forms: for example `pa`+copy(i) before i-initial stems and `po-` elsewhere. Analysis phonology leaves the vowels underspecified, so both allomorphs strip the same two segments of `pVVsa` and leave the same residue and the same state. They are siblings from one template slot, the battery and cascade dedup on `WordKey` (which carries the allomorph) keeps both, and the fold merges on the state key only after both subtrees were expanded. 68 of 224 rules have such a pair; 90 have two or more subrules. The earlier grammar census (the env-split-allomorph-fanout key: rule plus form plus environment) never groups different forms, so it measured a different set (4 same-form subrules). Twins cannot be merged in the grammar: they synthesise differently, so every twin arrival must be kept.
- **Three forms, and what each must reproduce.** Invariant (exact, subject to the audit below): for two nodes with equal state key and different trails, the stream under the second equals the stream under the first with the trail prefix replaced. (i) Positional twin replay inside one `apply_templates` call: expand the first twin once, record its fold events (canonical index or dropped, trail suffix; about 8 to 16 bytes per arrival), and re-send them through the same fold at the second twin's position, freeing the buffer at loop end and charging it to the word meter; at the cap, recompute. Exact (arrival order, alternatives, duplicates, root gate and `remaining` come out the same), no keyed table. (i') Pack twins on one node and expand the cross product lazily at the fold: NOT exact, because a fold by `WordKey` collision creates canonicals out of position and the root gate depends on timing. (ii) The stratum table (all classes, including rule order, the only form that pays on Aweti): a DAG of fold events keyed on the state key; this is a memo and needs the owner. Cap design drafted for form (ii): at most 131,072 rows and 16 MiB counted against the word memory meter (the largest measured call has 58,761 distinct hook states; the byte cap is an estimate), lifetime one stratum `analyze` call, no eviction, stop inserting at the cap while existing rows keep replaying, a miss always recomputes (exact at any cap state), each replayed event charges at least 1 work unit, rows published only for completed expansions, off under `--trace`, `--stats` and diagnostics.
- **Open soundness gap.** The audit of the invariant is not done: word fields outside the state key (`mpr`, `extras`, `obligatory`, `passed_over`, `non_head_app_index`) must be equal between the two nodes or be carried unread to arrivals, and the census checks counts only. Also required: no analysis reader of trail order or allomorph id (the count gate is the only one found) and no read of fold state by the subtree.
- **Tabling with sequence answers (literature frame).** Standard tabling returns each answer once and in a changed order; this engine needs every duplicate in the original order, so a row must hold the ordered answer sequence and replay must concatenate it at the consumer's position (the derivation-count semantics of Mumick et al. 1990 and Green et al. 2007, strengthened to sequences). A consumer arriving while the producer is unfinished must run in full or wait; the key must contain everything a continuation reads (a coarser key is call subsumption, rejected: [call-subsumption-tabling](README.md#call-subsumption-tabling)). Lead decision 2026-11-27: the stratum table is not built this round; synthesis comes first (see [synthesis-of-dying-variants](README.md#synthesis-of-dying-variants)).
- **Blowup it targets:** two unapplication orders reaching the same `AnalysisStateKey` are both expanded to the full subtree below, then the descendants are folded one by one as alternatives. Work multiplies by the number of orders (up to k! for k commuting unapplications), although the future of both is identical by the key's own contract (the key is what later gates read: syntactic feature structure, real feature structure, rule counts saturated at the maximum, morph history, template state).
- **Mechanism:** tabling (SLG or Earley deduction) over an interned state id. The first arrival at state S expands and records its continuation as a DAG of (state, delta) edges. A later arrival at S does not re-search; it is registered as an alternative of S's canonical, and descendants are materialised for it by grafting its prefix trail onto the stored deltas. `Word::expand_alternatives` already does this graft one stratum at a time; this generalises it to within-stratum parent links (a shared packed forest: OR-nodes are canonical plus alternatives, AND-nodes are shared continuations). In-progress states need completion handling or a fallback to full expansion; cycles are impossible only if gates stop at the maximum application count, which must be checked.
- **Where in PanGloss:** `rust/crates/pg-rules/src/cascade.rs` (`combination_rec`, `permutation_rec` take a table argument), `rust/crates/pg-rules/src/stratum.rs` (`run_mrule_cascade`, `apply_mrules`, `apply_templates`, the `fold` closure that owns the state words), `rust/crates/pg-rules/src/word.rs` (`expand_alternatives_impl`), `rust/crates/pg-rules/src/canonical.rs`, `rust/crates/pg-rules/src/analysis_state_key.rs`. In C#: `AnalysisStratumRule.Apply`, `CombinationRuleCascade.ApplyRules`, `Word.ExpandAlternatives`, and the removed `AnalysisScope.TryReplay` / `Word.ReplayOnto`.
- **Expected gain:** measured only on its predecessor: the removed memo gave 1.47x on Sena before the PriorityUnion port and 1.00x after (`docs/research/memo-sena-bisect.md`), and Aweti words needed it to finish under a step cap (`docs/research/memo-is-a-net-cost.md`). Expect large wins only on permutation-heavy words (Mbugwe and Aweti class), about zero elsewhere. The upper bound is the branch-census alias count versus nodes (`HC_BRANCH_CENSUS`); not yet run for this question.
- **Parity risk:** HIGH. (a) `WordKey` includes trail order, so reordered arrivals are distinct output words; the graft must reproduce the same set, first-seen order and alternative multiset including `dedup_alternative` pruning, `keeps_alternatives` root-gate decisions and the `max_unapplications` countdown. (b) The state key is only as sound as the audit of its readers (`analysis_state_key.rs` header); machine#594 shows how one missing reader breaks a key. (c) Work-cap behaviour changes. Gate: byte-identical outcomes and `duplicate_count` on all conformance grammars and 5,000-word samples.
- **Memo/cache?** Form (i), twin replay: no keyed table (a per-call buffer of fold events, capped, with an exact recompute fallback). Form (ii), the stratum table: YES. A per-word table of expanded continuations is a memo beyond the root trie and a revival of the removed memo's design (divergence 045). Needs owner approval before any prototype; the policy ranks it last.
- **Prior art:** Billot and Lang 1989, shared forests (https://aclanthology.org/P89-1018.pdf), the packing idea; Scott 2008, shared packed parse forests from Earley recognisers (https://pure.royalholloway.ac.uk/files/25270996/paperE.pdf), OR/AND packing of equal-state derivations; Chen and Warren 1996, SLG resolution (https://dblp.org/rec/journals/jacm/ChenW96), call variants share one table; Oepen and Carroll 2000 (https://aclanthology.org/A00-2022/), the caution that packed items must be unpacked with the exact feature structure; Pereira and Warren 1983 (cited from memory).
