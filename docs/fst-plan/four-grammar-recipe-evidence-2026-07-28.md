## Detailed gated case

The gated fixture is the informative positive case. Its baseline and two alternative recipes are executable, have distinct content-addressed roots where topology differs, and all preserve HC analysis identity and multiplicity. The report retains two Pareto-frontier roots and selects `e8367557975a9043` by the declared lexicographic policy. `report.json` stores the recipe label separately from the Plan-root ID; `baseline` and `winner` equal the roots in `baseline.plan.json` and `winner.plan.json`, which the integration test verifies.

The winner is exact only with respect to the three feasible candidates generated by registry schema 1 for this grammar and budget. It is not a universal claim about arbitrary Plan trees. Timings are measured evidence from this host, not portable thresholds.

## Negative and timeout evidence

The ordered and layered fixtures deliberately demonstrate the certification boundary: buildable candidates with real measurements remain ineligible when full HC finds a multiplicity mismatch. The deep template fixture demonstrates the hard process deadline: timeout produces an explicit non-certifying status and no false empty-result or winner claim. The adversarial `deletion-reduplication-exception-composite` fixture separately proves that every applicable distinct recipe preserves full-HC identity and multiplicity across deletion, full reduplication, and a lexical MPR exception.
